Quality & Engineering • Slide 3 of 5

Category Deep Dives & Quality Benchmarks

Advanced Ceramics

  • Dielectric Strength: > 15 kV/mm
  • Plasma Erosion: < 0.1 nm/min
  • Substrates: Al2O3, SiC, AlN, Quartz
  • Applications: E-Chucks, Etch Liners

Precision Machining

  • Vacuum Tightness: 1x10^-9 Torr
  • CMM Verification: 100% Sub-micron
  • Surface Finish: Ra < 0.2 µm
  • Materials: Al 6061-T6, 316L VIM/VAR

UHP Welding

  • Purge Argon: 6N (99.9999%)
  • Helium Leak: < 1x10^-10 atm·cc/s
  • Processes: Electron Beam, Orbital TIG
  • Applications: Gas Sticks, VMB Boxes

Cables & Interconnects

  • Cleanroom Rating: ISO Class 1 / 10
  • Outgassing TML: < 0.5% (ASTM E595)
  • Flex Cycles: > 10,000,000 cycles
  • Impedance: 50 Ω ± 2 Ω RF
