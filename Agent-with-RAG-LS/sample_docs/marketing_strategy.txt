# NexusCorp Annual Marketing Strategy & Go-To-Market Plan

1. Expand Enterprise Market Share by 35% across North America and Europe in FY2026.
2. Launch targeted campaign for Autonomous Agent Integration across Fintech and Healthcare verticals.
3. Accelerate organic lead conversion through technical webinars and case studies.

- **Project Vanguard**: Focus on AI transformation in legacy software systems.
- **OmniChannel Growth**: Digital advertising across LinkedIn, tech blogs, and developer hubs.
- **Partner Ecosystem**: Co-marketing initiatives with cloud infrastructure providers.
