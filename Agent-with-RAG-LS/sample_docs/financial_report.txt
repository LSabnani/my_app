# NexusCorp Q3 Financial Performance & Revenue Report

## Financial Summary
- **Gross Revenue**: $14.8 Million (22% YoY Increase)
- **Net Operating Profit**: $3.9 Million
- **Research & Development (R&D) Expense**: $2.6 Million
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**: Reduced by 14% to $1,850 per enterprise contract.

## Key Revenue Drivers
1. Enterprise Cloud Subscriptions accounted for 65% of total Q3 revenue.
2. AI Agent Integration & RAG Consulting Services contributed $3.2 Million.
3. Software Licensing and Maintenance agreements provided steady recurring cashflow.

## Q4 Financial Outlook
Projected Q4 revenue between $16.0M and $17.5M driven by expanding SaaS adoption and key year-end renewals.
